# PF Platform — Beta UAT Checklist (self-guided, by role)

**Goal:** each person spends ~20 minutes in the platform and sends Peter what they find. Async — no meeting needed. Target: open Beta this week.
**How to report:** just email Peter (peter@pierfoundations.com) a list — "X looks wrong," "Y is confusing," "Z is broken." Screenshots welcome.

---

## Everyone (2 min)
- [ ] Sign in at **pf-platform.pages.dev/login** with your own email + password. First time: use the temp password, then set your own.
- [ ] Does the overall look feel clean and professional / on-brand?
- [ ] Can you find things easily? Nav make sense?

## Brad — Admin (full access)
- [ ] Brand look and feel across the platform
- [ ] Dashboard shows the right KPIs for daily decisions
- [ ] Estimating markup structure correct (OH, commissions, contingency, profit)
- [ ] Nothing sensitive exposed that shouldn't be
- [ ] User management / roles make sense
- [ ] Anything that would confuse a new user

## Jonathan — Estimating & Project Management
- [ ] **Bid Pipeline** — project names, GCs, values, statuses match your bid log
- [ ] **Feasibility Tool** — scoring/thresholds make sense
- [ ] **Estimating** — POET template rates accurate (labor, equipment, stone, travel)
- [ ] **Active Projects / WIP** — matches the Project Master
- [ ] **QA/QC GUHMA + Modulus Testing** — thresholds and data right
- [ ] **Project records** — open a project, check the detail view, subcontract, budget vs actual
- [ ] Does the flow from bid invite → estimate → project → closeout fit how you actually work?

## Derek — Business Development
- [ ] **Dashboard** and overall navigation
- [ ] **Awarded Projects** + project records — accurate?
- [ ] The BD sections (Leads / Targets / Outreach) currently show "Coming in a later release" — confirm that's the right call and tell us what you'll want in them
- [ ] Anything BD-relevant that's missing or wrong
- [ ] Visual polish and clarity

## Field Crew — John, Seth, Jordan (Field Operations only)
- [ ] **Projects (field view)** — can you see your projects and their info?
- [ ] **Daily logs / Daily reports** — can you enter a daily?
- [ ] **TimeSheets** — can you enter time?
- [ ] **Safety** — accessible?
- [ ] Confirm you do NOT see financials, pricing, or contracts (you shouldn't — tell us if you do)
- [ ] Anything confusing for day-to-day field use
