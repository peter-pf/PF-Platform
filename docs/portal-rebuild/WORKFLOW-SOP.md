# Pier Foundations - Project Workflow SOP

*Peter’s current end-to-end understanding - DRAFT for Brad’s review and edits (2026-06-18)*


This captures the full lifecycle from opportunity to closeout, including the Peter automations as I understand them today. Mark it up however you like and I will fold your edits in.


## Phase 1 - Business Development & Preconstruction (Win the Work)

- Opportunity intake: a new opportunity is logged. Today it lives in the Project Bid Log; the plan is to enter it in the portal and have it backfill the Bid Log.
- Pipeline stages, tracked by the Bid Log section: Actively Bidding > Budget Pricing > Feasibility Review > Submitted Bids > Awarded / Not Awarded. Two disciplines: Aggregate Piers and Helical Piles.
- Feasibility Review (Jonathan owns): inputs are the geotech report and structural plans. Scoring: 100% aggregate piers within 175 miles of the yard scores highest; 100% AP within 350 miles next; mostly-AP with a little helical (building addition) within 175 miles lower. Pass on mixed-scope jobs beyond 300 miles or all-helical-only jobs for now. The larger the AP job, the farther we will travel. Output is a feasibility brief with a go / no-go verdict; Jonathan reviews and triggers whether to send to Garbin.
- Estimating & bid: Garbin prelim, takeoffs, proposal built on the PF bid template (page 2 lists submittal items needed; Exhibit A carries our standard terms - net-30, no retainage, no LDs, changed-site-conditions clause). Submit the bid and track it.

## Phase 2 - Award & Handoff

- On award (LOI / NTP) the job graduates out of Preconstruction and into the Project section: Project Management > Projects > [YY-NNN Project Name].
- Documents come over and populate the record: subcontract/contract, contract drawings, geotech, submittals. Brad forwards the contract to Peter; Peter adds it to the project and analyzes it (the subcontract tool, run per job), extracting scope, contract terms, insurance requirements, key dates, liquidated damages, contract duration, and total value into the record.
- Subcontract-award task list: send sample COI and insurance requirements to Certificates @ MJ and request the COI be issued to the GC; send a Project Safety Request to Safety Resources; once the COI is issued, check it against our PF COI and produce a gap report in that job. Capture project info fields (job name/address, GC and PM contacts, county/township, subcontract number and date, LOI date, bid invite/due dates).

## Phase 3 - Submittal Management (on LOI + GC release to start submittals)

- Peter emails Garbin (Jake) for the list of what GGG needs for full design; GGG responds.
- Peter relays the needed items to the GC project manager (cc the team). Submittal items needed: structural foundation plan (CAD), civil grading plan (CAD), full structural set (PDF), full civil set (PDF), design service loads (DL + LL), any additional geotech reports, and site grade elevation during PF install (within 3 inches). If PF does staking/layout, also a survey/site control (PDF) and civil site plan (CAD).
- Peter keeps a tracking checklist of returned vs outstanding, updates it daily, and emails the GC daily until everything is received.
- When complete, Peter compiles the package and emails the Garbin team (Jake & Projects), requesting the prelim back no later than 3 weeks from send. That date is tracked in the project schedule and log.
- When Garbin returns the design, Peter forwards it to the GC submittal manager (cc Brad, Jonathan, Derek, Ray) and tracks the dates sent.
- GC review runs ~1-2 weeks; Peter sends a reminder after one week. On return the comments go to Brad/Jonathan/Derek/Ray.
- If Approved/Reviewed: file in two places (PM approved drawings and the Field drawings folder), email the installing crew, send out for print, and log both dates. If Revise & Resubmit: send to Garbin for revision (ask for an ETA), then resubmit to the GC and await approval.

## Phase 4 - Pre-Mobilization (Site & PF Readiness)

- Safety: obtain the Site Specific Safety Plan (SSSP) from Progressive Safety. Prepare toolbox talks, JSA, MSDS, daily equipment checklist, and hand-log sheets.
- Utility locates: call in locates (Ohio = OUPS / 811, at least 2 working days before digging). Peter prepares the ticket; a person submits it; Peter logs the ticket number, the cleared-to-dig date, and the expiration, and renews before it lapses.
- GC site readiness: building pad passes proof roll, pad elevation at staking confirmed, firm and level working pad capable of the 120,000 lb rig.
- Equipment: PF mobilization (predrill rig, vibro rig, parts and fall-off loads). Rentals (track loader, mini excavator, telehandler, compressor) - one week before start Peter sets up delivery (site contact John Willis), confirms two days prior, and tracks dates and rates against the national account.
- Materials: stone (approved and ordered), helical material if any, and fuel (tank/containment and delivery).
- Testing setup: Peter populates the modulus test document per Jonathan’s SOP and saves it to the proper folder before work starts.

## Phase 5 - Mobilization & Installation

- Mobilize to site; Field Operations Manager does a jobsite visit with the GC.
- Install aggregate piers / helical piles at design locations.
- Daily: the crew emails the GUHMA data and hand logs to Peter, Derek, Jonathan, Brad, and Ray. Peter runs the log check (including the mislabel number-fit check) and updates percent complete, driven by linear feet installed. This repeats daily until 100%.
- Modulus load testing performed per GGG/PF direction.

## Phase 6 - Demobilization & Closeout

- On completion (John emails the team the job is finished): call off fuel delivery, call off stone deliveries and request final invoices, schedule equipment pickup/demob/transport to the next job, call off all rentals and request final invoices, and perform column checks. All referenced by job number.
- As-builts: Peter sends Garbin the modulus results, hand logs, and GUHMA data, requesting as-builts back in 14 days. On return, Peter checks them against our records (per Jonathan’s SOP). If they match, send to the GC; if not, send Garbin the discrepancies for revision before sending to the GC.
- Closeout: send the closeout package and column logs to the GC; file approved drawings.

## Phase 7 - Financials (runs throughout)

- Pay applications (AIA G702/G703) per the billing schedule, with lien waivers, invoiced by the contract due date.
- Budget vs Actual job-cost tracking by cost code, run in parallel with QuickBooks and reconciled to it.
- Retainage: the final retainage pay application is triggered once all pay apps to date are paid and the as-builts have been sent, and must be sent by the due date in the subcontract.
- Vendor invoices coded to the correct job number and cost code; job cost report maintained.

## Cross-Cutting

- Project log: every email and document transfer (date and time) is logged into the project history for tracking.
- Dashboard: subcontract risk items are surfaced on the CEO/COO dashboard and click through to the exact contract language flagged as a risk exposure.
- Single source: everything lives in the portal, documents embedded and viewable inline, and edits in the portal backfill the source spreadsheets (phased - read from Excel first, then turn on write-back).
