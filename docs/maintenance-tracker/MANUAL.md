# User Manual: Maintenance Tracker

**For:** Field crew and Field Operations users
**Feature:** Maintenance checklist and manual items
**Status:** Live

---

## What it does

Maintenance is now its own tab in Field Operations. It gathers every maintenance item and future issue you have noted in your daily reports into one checklist, grouped by equipment. You check items off as you fix them, and you can also add maintenance items by hand that did not come from a daily report.

## Where to find it

Look for **Maintenance** in the Field Operations menu. It used to live under Daily Reports. It is now its own top-level item, right alongside Projects.

## How the checklist is organized

Items are grouped by equipment category:

- Excavator
- Mast
- Vibro
- Drill
- Rental Equipment
- Future Maintenance Items (a separate section for issues flagged for later)

Each item that came from a daily report shows the project, the date, and the foreman who reported it, so you know where it came from.

## Checking an item off

1. Find the item in its category.
2. Tap the checkbox.

That is it. The platform records that the item is done, who did it, and when, automatically. Completed items fade and slide into a **Completed** sub-group inside that category so the list stays clean and you only see what is left.

## Adding a maintenance item by hand

Some maintenance does not start from a daily report. To add one yourself:

1. Open the Manual Add form.
2. Pick the **Maintenance type** (one of the 5 categories).
3. Pick the **Issue type**: Failure or Maintenance.
4. Enter a description.
5. Optionally enter the equipment or subcategory.
6. Save.

Your manual item joins the same checklist in the right category. You check it off the same way.

## Removing a manual item

Only items you added by hand can be removed (they are tagged "manual"). A Remove option appears on those rows. Items that came from daily reports cannot be removed here, since they are part of the report record.

## Good to know

- **Completion sticks.** Once you check something off it stays checked across sessions and for everyone on the team.
- **Who and when are automatic.** You do not type your name or the date. The platform fills that in from your login.
- **No prices anywhere.** Maintenance has no financial data, by design.
