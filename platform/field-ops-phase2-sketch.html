Field Ops AI Companion Phase 2 Sketch

For Tether (review and markup) · From Peter, Pier Foundations · Phase 1 is live in production

The goal

A field site lead asks a question in plain English and gets a short, correct answer drawn from the real project records. Benchmark case from Jonathan: "who is my stone vendor for POET?" returns the vendor and the contact, not a guess.

The non-negotiable

The field role sees zero financials. No dollar figures, unit prices, contract values, budgets, or margins ever reach the field surface. This holds structurally, not as a filter we hope catches everything.

Architecture: two layers, not two choices

Phase 1 (LIVE today) — in-browser synonym and intent search. Direct lookups, instant, no round trip. Reads the field-safe index.
↓ only if Phase 1 cannot resolve
Phase 2 — synchronous call to the PureBrain governed Q&A endpoint. PureBrain holds project memory and enforces role policy, retrieves from field-safe records, and composes one grounded sentence.
↓ only on low confidence
Fallback — async route to a human (or to me). The safety net, not the default path.

Field crews need speed, so Phase 1 stays the instant first pass and Phase 2 is synchronous when it fires.

The data boundary (the heart of it)

The model never touches raw data. It asks the governed layer, which retrieves from field-safe records and composes the answer.

Field role may see: vendors (company, scope, contact, phone, email, website) with pricing scrubbed, site and PF contacts, approved materials labels and document links, schedule milestones (dates and durations), safety links, QA/QC counts.

It must never receive: any dollar amount or unit price, contract or subcontract value, budget, margin, or GC commercial terms. Phase 2 reuses the same money-scrubbed view Phase 1 already produces, so there is one source of truth for what the field can see.

Request and response sketch

Request from the platform to the governed endpoint:

{
  "question": "who is my stone vendor for POET?",
  "role": "field_ops",
  "project_hint": "26-002",
  "context_scope": "field_safe"
}

Response we would render:

"Your stone vendor for POET is Rush County Stone Company. Contact Carleigh at 765-629-2211 or Mike.Malinoff@jrjnet.com."

Returned with a confidence score (drives the human fallback) and a contains-financials flag I would treat as a hard block that never renders, defense in depth on top of the policy layer.
